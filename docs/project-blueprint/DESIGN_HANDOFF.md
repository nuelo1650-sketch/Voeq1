# Voeq — Design Exploration Handoff (honest account)

> Written for handoff. This is NOT a finished design system. It is an honest record of what was
> explored, what it's worth, and why it did not land. Use it to redirect a human designer or a
> tighter brief.

## 1. What was actually asked
- Voeq = campus marketplace (Nigeria, pilot NMU). Product/architecture already specified separately.
- **Creative direction exploration only** — no code, no final design system.
- Locked constraints from founder: forest green + muted gold + cream palette; editorial + modern +
  minimal + campus-native + premium + "alive"; responsive; smooth animation; WhatsApp removed
  (native messaging instead).
- References supplied: Silencio (restraint/silence-as-material) and destroytoday (monograph, color as
  punctuation). Also 6 generic AI-cliché templates the founder said "look ok" (these are the AVOID
  list, not the target).
- Explicit later instruction: **"focus on background for landing page than plain cream."**

## 2. What was produced (all in docs/project-blueprint/)
- `01-CREATIVE_DIRECTION_EXPLORATION.md` — the main doc (A–D directions + M1–M3 modern + BG-1/BG-2
  background landings).
- `explorations/*.html` — 13 rendered, screenshot-verified prototype pages:
  - A (Broadsheet), B (Green Room), C (Living Index), D (Contour Field)
  - M1 (Vitrine/light), M2 (Dark Gallery), M3 (Editorial Object)
  - BG-terra, BG-darkfield (background-first landing pages)
  - plus vendor/messaging/discovery signature screens.

## 3. Honest assessment — why it does not feel designed
1. **Option-board, not vision.** I produced breadth (7+ directions) instead of one bold, well-executed
   bet. A founder who wants to *love* something gets more from one killer execution than ten competent
   variations on a muted theme.
2. **Generic stock photography.** Every prototype used Unsplash placeholder images (pizza, portraits,
   group shots). Premium/minimal aesthetics live or die on *art-directed imagery*; stock photos are the
   fastest way to make a page feel like a template. This alone sank the "premium" feel.
3. **The background phenomenon is too timid.** The founder asked for the background to be a hero. I built
   a topographic contour + vendor-constellation concept (genuinely Voeq-specific: campus terrain +
   live vendors as dots) but kept it at 6–9% opacity for legibility — so it reads as "subtle texture,"
   not the striking phenomenon requested. Correct for usability, wrong for the stated desire.
4. **Safe execution of tight constraints.** The locked palette + editorial+minimal brief pushed every
   direction into the same muted, quiet register. Without a strong art-directed concept, they converge
   into "tasteful template" rather than something with edge or feeling.
5. **No trained visual taste loop.** The agent can apply design-methodology skills (surface-first,
   slop-audit, type/color systems) and verify renders, but cannot *guarantee* a feeling of beauty the
   way a trained designer's eye can. That limitation is real and showed in the output.

## 4. What is genuinely reusable (don't throw all of it away)
- The **contour + vendor-constellation background concept** (BG-1/BG-2) is a real, unique idea worth
  keeping — it just needs to be executed bolder (higher contrast, real campus data, art-directed).
- The **"modern = Silencio discipline"** translation (extreme type contrast, one accent, no shadow,
  floating artifacts) is a sound principle.
- The **native-messaging spec** and **unified mobile nav** decisions are sound product calls.
- The palette is fine; the problem is execution, not the colors.

## 5. What a human designer (or a better pass) would need
- A **single, specific reference the founder actually loves** (not "these look ok" — one hero image).
- **Art-directed photography** or a defined imagery direction, not stock placeholders.
- Permission to make the background **bold** (the founder wants it to be a phenomenon, not subtle).
- One **opinionated direction** to execute deeply, not a menu of options.
- Possibly loosening the "minimal" lock if the founder actually wants something with more energy.

## 6. Open questions still unanswered
- Exact green/gold/cream shades (locked as a family, not values).
- Whether "alive" should come from motion or from live data (founder rejected generic ambient motion).
- Photography direction (campus lifestyle? product-only? illustrated?).
- Light vs dark as the primary surface.

## 7. Status
Nothing approved. No design system. No code. Exploration only — and, per the founder's own read, not
yet at a quality worth building on. Recommend: either engage a human designer with this brief, or
restart with one hero reference + art-directed imagery + permission to go bold on the background.
