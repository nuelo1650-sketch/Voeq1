## Cross-reference synthesis (what Voeq takes)
- **Search is the hero** (Amazon/Airbnb/ClassifiAds): composite pill search — keyword + campus + category + submit — above the fold, full-width on mobile.
- **Mixed-grade grid is the noise killer** (Depop/boats/pools/NovaTrend): 2× feature cards w/ scrim overlays (price, rating, category chip) alternating with standard cards. Hierarchy = curation.
- **Trust strip under hero** (Amazon ×2): campus-verified, meet-on-campus safety, real students, Voeq protected — Voeq's versions.
- **Location context in nav** (boats/Amazon): campus switcher persistent.
- **Social proof layered** (enterprise/NovaTrend): ratings + saves + "active vendors" — REAL data only.
- **Badges with strict semantics** (NovaTrend/Amazon): Featured (gold), New, Trending — one meaning per color, consistent placement.
- **Roll/marquee animation + band rhythm** (founder ask): alternate full-width editorial bands with card rails so the eye never fatigues.
- **Supply-side CTA elevated** (ClassifiAds): "Sell on Voeq" gets a real place (nav + footer), not a scappy banner.
