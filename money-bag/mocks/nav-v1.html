Landing nav — three calmer directions

Your call from the phone screenshot: the two-row app-chrome (search pill + quick links + Sell button) is too much for the landing. The landing's job is one feeling and one button — search and browsing live on /explore. Each variant below shows desktop and 390px mobile. Same hero everywhere so you can judge the nav against real context.

A — Whisper

One row, four elements: logo, Explore, Sign in, Get started. Nothing else. The sell path survives once as text inside the hero ("or sell on Voeq") and in the footer — it doesn't need a button at the top of a page whose whole job is selling it. Maximum editorial calm; smallest footprint.

The most opinionated: the nav carries only brand + Sign in + Get started. Explore/Live/sell are all below (hero CTA + ExploreDoor + footer). The landing becomes a pure advertisement page — a single entrance. Risk: fewer ways around; upside: nothing competes with the one button that matters.

What all three share: single row, no search pill (that's explore's job), no quick-links ticker, no loud Sell pill competing with the primary CTA, real BrandLogo, signed-in users get working heart/message icons, mobile collapses to logo + Get started + burger drawer (drawer = Explore / How it works / Voeq Live / Sell). My recommendation: B — it keeps one quiet sell route at the top without a second button fighting for attention, and "How it works" earns its place for cold visitors. A if you want it even quieter. C only if you believe the landing should be a one-door page.
