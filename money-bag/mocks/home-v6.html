What is Voeq?

The market for Nigerian students — a place to discover who sells what around you, talk to them directly, and get it without wahala. Built for campuses, open to every corner of Nigeria.

01Find it

Search the market or browse stalls by category. Every vendor is campus-verified — real people, real businesses, no guesswork.

02Chat it

Message vendors right here on Voeq. Ask questions, agree a price, agree where to meet — no WhatsApp juggling.

03Get it

Meet on campus or get it delivered. You pay when you're happy — that's how a trusted market works.
