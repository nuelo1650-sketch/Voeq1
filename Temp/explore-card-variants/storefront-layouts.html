Storefront — three arrangements

All three fix the same six defects: category NAMES not ids, no lede duplication, listings moved ABOVE about, one Message CTA (hero), socials as chips, share folded into a slim bar. They differ in how much the hero dominates.

S1 — "Goods first"

Compact identity header (avatar, name, trust, category pills, one-line description, Contact + Follow, social chips) → stat bar → LISTINGS immediately → reviews → related → share bar. The About card is GONE — the description lives in the header once. Most marketplace-standard.

My ranking: S1 > S2 > S3. S1 is the marketplace-standard arrangement — identity, proof, goods, reviews, share — with zero duplication and one Message CTA. S2 is the most beautiful but puts text on an image (the legibility gamble you rejected on cards, at 12× the size) and depends on vendors having a good cover photo — many don't yet. S3 is the fastest to browse but the coldest; the action tiles also duplicate what the hero already does. S1 with S2's stat-bar treatment is where I'd land.
